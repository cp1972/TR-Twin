# Course configuration — deepening the main finding

**Setup.** Load together, in the twin: the actor cohort (`cohorte_contrat.csv`) and the full
instance cohort (`instances_full_empirical.csv`, which carries both mediation instances — driving
the field — and control instances — `categorie=controle`, auto-routed to control capacity).
Everything moves at once: actor circulation, instance force, control capacity, reciprocity.

**Deepening question.** The main finding was that these working-class autobiographers are
*atypical by destination* — they reach other relational structures (Culture A, Media D) — yet
*typical* once one remembers how few workers had such a fate. The question: **what role did the
mediation and control instances play in this atypical transversal circulation?**

**Result (see `course_transversal.svg`).** For the two destination structures, the working-class
*presence* (the crossing materialising) builds up *together with* the structure's instances:

- **A · Culture:** corr(presence, mediation) = **0.81**, corr(presence, control) = **0.97**.
- **D · Media:**   corr(presence, mediation) = **0.63**, corr(presence, control) = **0.88**.

The crossing into A and D is tightly coupled to the build-up of those structures' instances, and
*most* tightly to the **control apparatus** (certification and criticism in A; gatekeeping and
economic evaluation in D). Read in TR terms: the workers do not cross into a vacuum — they enter
through the mediations, and above all through the control instances that sort, certify and
consecrate. The instances are the *channels* of the atypical crossing; the control apparatus is
what makes the destination both reachable (a graded ladder to climb) and absorbing (it keeps the
structure reactive as incomers arrive). Reciprocity of the destination declines as it fills,
which is the cost side of the same process.

Correlations are computed over the years where presence > 0; series come from a deterministic
replay of the twin (actors + mediation + control).
