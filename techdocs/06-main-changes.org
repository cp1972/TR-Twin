# Status: version 62 (v62). Living document.

* Version history (selection)

- *v1–v11:* Construction of the base model — relational structures and sequences, chains, regime, pump/feedback/mirroring, inter-structure coupling, Gini, aesthetics.
- *v12:* Simplified chronology (always keyframe-driven), speed control, sequences arranged in a ring around the core.
- *v13:* Progressive (smoothed) rupture instead of abrupt disintegration; geometric speed control; CSV import.
- *v14:* Migration of actors (mass-conserving), sclerosis condition for the structure switch.
- *v15:* Reproducible random seed; visualisation of migration (golden dots).
- *v16–v17:* Migration target and declassing clarified according to the theory: arrival in the core of the target structure, declassed; declassing control.
- *v18:* Increased hysteresis margin against regime flickering (which previously stifled the dynamism).
- *v19:* Demographics per relational structure (weight + composition), anchoring, A/B/C/D selection, new CSV format.
- *v20:* Relational dynamism principle (influence to the most mobile relational structure), weaker anchoring and relaxation — the relational size can now deviate strongly from the population.
- *v21:* Plain-language naming of all controls and displays; theoretical term in each case as a tooltip; Established/Disappointed instead of possessing/excluded.
- *v22:* Non-deviant default values per control (displayed), symmetric start state; tooltips unified in French.
- *v23:* Flight of the disappointed (migration of the fourth category); reciprocity as a measured curve, shown together with inequality in the central panel.
- *v24:* Refined defaults of the healthy state: declassing 0.5 and "advantage for dynamic structures" 0.5 (active mobility, but without effect in the symmetric start).
- *v25:* Mediation instances, stage 1: force per type (relation/sequence/sub-sequence), fed by the processed actor flow; strengthening, weakening, destruction; effect on reciprocity; health display per structure.
- *v26:* Stage 2: power matrix — the health of each structure is the weighted mean of its instances, so that each structure becomes vulnerable where its strength lies.
- *v27:* Stage 3: degraded relay — structural problem in a cascade, strengthening then exhaustion of the taking-over instance (energy reserve), control instance that re-regulates; display of the "unregulated tension" that diminishes reciprocity.
- *v28:* Physical fate, descending coupling: mediation dampens or accelerates the fatigue (factor gm). Good mediation carries, unregulated tension accelerates the disintegration.
- *v29:* Ascending coupling — profiled forces (PT), ascending coupling (deliberately weak in C), threshold expansion (Pareto), control that solves the remainder. The four structures diverge for the first time; the perfect state is preserved (threshold 1.15).
- *v30:* Display of the three instance forces per structure (relation/sequence/sub-sequence) instead of a capped mean; force ceiling raised from 1.7 to 2.5, so that the lower instance is no longer artificially plateaued — the profiles (especially those of C) become legible and respond to the controls.
- *v31:* Fourth display bar per structure for the engagement of control above its rest value (high in D with simultaneously low tension — the evidence of successful regulation).
- *v32:* Satellisation I: the distress of marginalisation. Satellisation (deficit × dominance) feeds the structural problem (the cascade and control of the marginalised structures kick in) and erodes the internal solidity (reversible). Reciprocity and inequality are now read two-sidedly — no longer only from the perspective of expansion.
- *v33:* Satellisation II (active): absorption (capped by a lower bound) and continuous orbit of the satellite toward the dominant; ruler debouncing, which resolves the limit cycle driven by ruler flickering (end of the visible jumping of the relational structures).
- *v34:* Reorganisation of the interface: central evaluation panel at the top left (evolution curve, global bars, instances per structure); title "Theory of the Relation. Digital Twin"; removal of the "Shake/Draw" buttons and of the separate trajectory.
- *v35:* Permeability between structures: explicit diffusion of the inner composition toward the cross-structural mean (KDIFF = 1.5), whereby the control "over" acts visibly; zero at the perfect state.
- *v36:* Terminological unification of the interface: "structure (relationnelle)" instead of "structure", "séquence" instead of "sous-structure", "fragments séquences" instead of "morceau"; the misleading control "internal circulation" is clarified to "assimilation".
- *v37:* Fine-tuning of the labels: control "actors circulation" (rate, distinguished from "migration of actors"); legend of the mini-curve restored (Gini white / reciprocity turquoise); the red bar "tension" becomes "overload".
- *v38:* Gini and reciprocity per structure: own reciprocity R[x] and internal Gini G[x] per structure, as a refinement of the global measures; verified headless (R = 1 at the perfect state).
- *v39:* Contributions to the global level: signed contribution of each structure to inequality (Σ = global Gini) and contribution to the weakening of reciprocity (Σ = global deficit) — it becomes visible that the dominant weakens systemic reciprocity the most, although its own is the highest. The central panel is now a horizontal strip of three blocks (global / instances / per structure).
- *v40:* Controller profiles: the twelve mechanism controls can be governed over time via a CSV (linear interpolation, label as alias), governed controls marked turquoise. Verified headless: correct interpolation, a neutral profile reproduces the manual run, time-varying controls change the dynamism stably, the perfect state is preserved.
- *v41:* Theory profiles with chain coupling: a profile can also set the chains (row "chaine_X,…") and thus carry a complete theory configuration. Use case: the "dialectised Bourdieu" profile couples A↔C directly (short-circuit of the mediation via D and B) at high permeability — the operationalisation of capital convertibility.
- *v42:* Persistence of the profile chains: set chains now survive every reset and the start of the chronology (previously the timeline start reset the chains and thereby deleted the premise). A profile without chains explicitly establishes the standard chains of the Theory of the Relation.
- *v43:* Selective plasticity per structure: a chain declared in the profile is fixed (posited premise), the others remain plastic. Thus A and C can be held rigid (dialectised) while B and D continue to reorient — theoretically fruitful (see the "Methodology" document).
- *v44:* Actor layer: import of a real cohort (contract table) as demographics, step interpolation, switch linear/steps. Verified headless (aggregation, conservation of stocks).
- *v45:* Circulation flows: 4×4 matrix of the structure switches in a sliding window, living along the chronology — the carried paths become visible.
- *v46:* Enlarged, more legible flow matrix.
- *v47:* Removal of the "start size" control from the interface (the symmetric start remains internal); tidying up before the outputs.
- *v48:* Result outputs: deterministic replay of the loaded configuration from beginning to end; CSV of the time series (sizes, Gini/reciprocity/overload, per structure own reciprocity, internal Gini, contributions, ruler, satellisation, as well as the 16 columns of the category composition) and CSV of the actor transitions (long format); five print-ready SVG figures (sizes, Gini/reciprocity, per structure, flows, internal mobility).
- *v49:* Correction of the SVG output: escaping of the XML special characters (&, <, >) in titles and labels; accents in the figures restored.
- *v50:* Sequence-stake layer: the contract table is enriched by a column "sequence" (actor = structure + sequence + category); the transition export additionally captures the sequence changes (struct/seq/cat, long format); an aggregated figure shows the sequence-stake composition per structure over the whole period. Kept deliberately descriptive, not model-governing (thin cohort). Verified headless: 119 transitions = 58 structure + 21 sequence + 40 category.
- *v51:* Correction of the timeline end: at the end of the chronology the run is stopped cleanly (running = false, reset, play button reset) — no more accidental drifting on.
- *v52–v53:* Internationalisation (DE/EN/FR): infrastructure (a trilingual dictionary, language switch, pill, English default) and translation of the entire interface — central strip, control labels and tooltips, structure names, actor categories, regime note, legends.
- *v54–v55:* Completion of the translation (import buttons, checkboxes, sequence detail, legends, status messages) and pedagogical revision of the control tooltips for first-time users.
- *v56:* Localisation of the outputs: file names, titles, and legends of the SVG figures as well as status messages follow the language; the CSV files are prefixed with a UTF-8 BOM (correct umlauts in Excel).
- *v57:* Fixed English CSV column headers (language-independently stable for analysis) with localised file names; Solarized colour palette for the six SVG figures (light cream ground, Solarized accents, axes and text in muted tones).
- *v58:* Actor categories as data values in the transitions follow the language (Etablierte/… or established/…), the column headers stay English; adaptive title size in the line charts (long titles shrink, so that "per structure" stays fully visible).
- *v59:* Cascade of the three inequality levels: the finer resolution of inequality, only planned up to v58, is implemented as a measurement layer — transversal (between the structures, = global Gini), horizontal (between the sequences of a structure, = mean of the internal Ginis), and vertical (new: status gap between the four actor categories within a sequence, as a size-weighted status Gini, globally as well as per structure). New block in the central strip with the vertical inequality per structure and the reading "the apex homogenises, the dominated stratify". A pure display from the existing state (no new force); not yet carried in the result outputs.
- *v60:* What-if scenarios ("et si"), stage 1: a no-code scenario panel. Trends are derived /deterministically/ from the loaded history (presence from the structural shares, control from the capacity floor — read directly from the drivers, no stochastic replay). A scenario is a historical trend × a transformation chosen from a trilingual verb menu (continue / accelerate / dampen / reverse / freeze / redirect X→Y) × a horizon. A deterministic readout contrasts the starting state (last historical year) with the projection at the horizon and states the consequence in plain language (channel widens/narrows, inequality up/down, stratification up/down). Scenarios can be saved as cards and replayed. Guiding principle: a scenario = historical trend × transformation × horizon, never code.
- *v61:* What-if scenarios, stage 2 — the scenario plays on the timeline. The projection is written into the frieze itself: presence as an /additive share shift/ (robust even when the target structure's share is tiny, as in the A-Culture-concentrated cohort tail), control as a live capacity ramp read every frame. The timeline is extended by the horizon and playback runs from a few years before the pivot into the projected tail (warm-started so the simulation state is correct at the entry year), so the structures visibly move. Magnitude and horizon are now effective levers (previously indexed on the faint historical slope, below display precision). A reset button restores the historical frieze; the readout and the cards move to the central panel, beside the circulation flows. Verified headless: frieze extends, magnitude and horizon change the projection, control scenario, determinism, reset, card replay, trilingual.
- *v62:* Scenario SVG export and unified figure aesthetic. A print-ready figure of the projected trajectory (reciprocity, transversal Gini, target stratification, with a dashed pivot marker and the trend × transformation × horizon as subtitle). The six in-app figure exports (sizes, Gini/reciprocity, per structure, flows, internal mobility, sequence-stake) are restyled from the earlier Solarized-cream look to the same white-ground, Inter-set publication aesthetic as the course figures: hairline panels and grids, monospace axis ticks, line-swatch legends, footers. Verified headless: all figures well-formed, white ground, no legacy palette residue.
