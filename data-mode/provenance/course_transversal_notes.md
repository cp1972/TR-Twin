# Course configuration — deepening the main finding

**Setup.** Load together, in the twin: the actor cohort (`cohorte_contrat.csv`) and the full
instance cohort (`instances_full_empirical.csv`, which carries both mediation instances — driving
the field — and control instances — `categorie=controle`, auto-routed to control capacity, now with
real **authority curves**). Everything moves at once.

**Deepening question.** The main finding: these working-class autobiographers are *atypical by
destination* (they reach Culture A and Media D) yet *typical* once one remembers how few workers
had such a fate. What carried that crossing? The question: **what role did the mediation and
control instances play?**

**Result (see `course_transversal.svg`).** For the two destination structures, the working-class
*presence* (the crossing materialising) is tightly coupled to the instances — but the two
structures differ in a way that is itself the finding:

- **D · Media:** corr(presence, mediation) = **0.63**, corr(presence, **control**) = **0.98**.
  The control apparatus of Media (Reuters from 1851, the Financial Times from 1888) was *built
  co-temporally with the workers entering media*. They rose together: an **emerging sphere**, its
  control instances and its working-class entrants co-constituted.
- **A · Culture:** corr(presence, mediation) = **0.81**, corr(presence, **control**) = **0.78**.
  Culture's certification apparatus (the ancient universities, then University College, then the
  examining University of London) *largely pre-existed*. The workers climbed an **already-built
  ladder**, opened to autodidacts at key points — above all the University of London's external
  degrees (1858). Here mediation tracks the crossing more closely than control, because control
  was already in place.

**Reading.** The crossing is mediated throughout, but the relation to *control* tells two
histories: in Media, control and crossing are **co-built** (the sphere is being born); in Culture,
control **pre-exists** and the crossing is an *ascent* of an established apparatus. This sharpens
the main result: the atypical transversal circulation is not an escape into a vacuum but an entry
through instances — co-emerging with them in D, scaling them in A.

Correlations are computed over the years where presence > 0; series come from a deterministic
replay of the twin (actors + mediation + control). Control authority curves are sourced for the
structuring instances (see `control_parc_empirical_notes.md`).
