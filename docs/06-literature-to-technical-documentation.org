* Literature

The following titles are cited in the text — both for the theoretical arguments and for the provenance of the mathematical model building blocks.

- *Ashby, W. Ross (1956):* An Introduction to Cybernetics. Chapman & Hall, London.
- *Boudon, Raymond (1977):* Effets pervers et ordre social. Presses Universitaires de France, Paris.
- *Deleuze, Gilles (1990):* Post-scriptum sur les sociétés de contrôle. In: Pourparlers 1972–1990. Minuit, Paris, pp. 240–247.
- *Durkheim, Émile (1893):* De la division du travail social. Alcan, Paris.
- *DiMaggio, Paul J. / Powell, Walter W. (1983):* The Iron Cage Revisited: Institutional Isomorphism and Collective Rationality in Organizational Fields. American Sociological Review 48 (2), pp. 147–160.
- *Frank, André Gunder (1967):* Capitalism and Underdevelopment in Latin America. Monthly Review Press, New York.
- *Gini, Corrado (1912):* Variabilità e mutabilità. Tipografia di Paolo Cuppini, Bologna.
- *Gouldner, Alvin W. (1960):* The Norm of Reciprocity: A Preliminary Statement. American Sociological Review 25 (2), pp. 161–178.
- *Gramsci, Antonio (1929–1935):* Quaderni del carcere. Ed. V. Gerratana, Einaudi, Turin 1975.
- *Habermas, Jürgen (1962):* Strukturwandel der Öffentlichkeit. Luchterhand, Neuwied.
- *Hegel, Georg Wilhelm Friedrich (1820):* Grundlinien der Philosophie des Rechts. Nicolai, Berlin.
- *Mauss, Marcel (1925):* Essai sur le don. Forme et raison de l'échange dans les sociétés archaïques. L'Année Sociologique, n.s. 1, pp. 30–186.
- *Merton, Robert K. (1936):* The Unanticipated Consequences of Purposive Social Action. American Sociological Review 1 (6), pp. 894–904.
- *Papilloud, Christian / Schultze, Eva-Maria (2022):* Skizze einer Theorie der Relation. Springer VS, Wiesbaden (ISBN 978-3-658-37922-3).
- *Pareto, Vilfredo (1909):* Manuel d'économie politique. Giard & Brière, Paris (first in Italian, 1906).
- *Pareto, Vilfredo (1916):* Trattato di sociologia generale. Barbèra, Florence (circulation of elites).
- *Reckwitz, Andreas (2017):* Die Gesellschaft der Singularitäten. Suhrkamp, Berlin.
- *Schmoller, Gustav von (1900/1904):* Grundriß der Allgemeinen Volkswirtschaftslehre. 2 vols. Duncker & Humblot, Leipzig.
- *Schumpeter, Joseph A. (1942):* Capitalism, Socialism and Democracy. Harper & Brothers, New York.
- *Strogatz, Steven H. (1994):* Nonlinear Dynamics and Chaos. Addison-Wesley, Reading (MA).
- *Verhulst, Pierre-François (1838):* Notice sur la loi que la population suit dans son accroissement. Correspondance mathématique et physique 10, pp. 113–121.
- *Wallerstein, Immanuel (1974):* The Modern World-System I: Capitalist Agriculture and the Origins of the European World-Economy in the Sixteenth Century. Academic Press, New York.
- *Weber, Max (1919):* Politik als Beruf. Duncker & Humblot, Munich/Leipzig.
