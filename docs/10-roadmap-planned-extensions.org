* 10. Planned extensions

Implemented up to v59: mediation instances complete, satellisation, permeability, Gini and reciprocity per structure together with contributions, the cascade of the three inequality levels (transversal, horizontal, vertical; Section 5.17), time-varying controller profiles, theory profiles with chain coupling and selective plasticity, the actor layer (cohort demographics in steps and circulation flows), the result outputs (CSV time series, actor transitions, print-ready SVG figures), as well as the descriptive sequence-stake layer (Section 9.5). Since v52, the full trilingualism of the interface and the outputs has also been implemented (Section 6.1). Before the comparison report, the following essentially remain:

- *Result outputs (implemented, v48–v50):* deterministic replay, CSV of the trajectories and of the actor transitions (with sequence), six print-ready SVG figures in a Solarized palette — the raw material of the report.
- *Trilingualism (implemented, v52–v58):* interface and outputs in German, English, and French with English default; fixed English CSV column headers and Solarized figures (Section 6.1).
- *Inequality cascade (implemented, v59):* the three inequality levels of the theory (transversal, horizontal, vertical) as a display in the central strip (Section 5.17). Open follow-up: their inclusion in the deterministic result outputs (one column per level in the time series, one SVG figure of the three levels over time), so that the vertical stratification of the dominated can also be quantified and illustrated.
- *Prism mode (horizon):* the system in the prism of a single actor or instance (Section 9.1); here the actor tracer gains its meaning (N = 1–5).
- *Full state Bourdieu (open):* B-mediated A↔C convertibility as a counter-test to the direct dialectisation (Section 9.2a); furthermore a term of mass convertibility A↔C (the mass of A does not yet grow along under dialectisation).

This document is updated with each step.
